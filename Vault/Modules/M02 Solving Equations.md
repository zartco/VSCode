## The balance idea
An equation is a balance: the left side equals the right side. To keep it true, **whatever you do to one side you do to the other**. That single rule drives everything. See [[Properties of Equality]].

## The legal moves
Add, subtract, multiply, or divide both sides by the same number (never divide by 0); plus [[Distributive Property|distribute]] and combine [[Like Terms|like terms]].

## The general strategy
OpenStax's five steps solve *any* linear equation:

![[final_strategy.png]]

In plain terms: **simplify each side → gather variables on one side → gather numbers on the other → divide to get $x$ alone → check.**

> [!example]- Example A — two-step: $2x+5=13$
> Subtract 5: $2x=8$. Divide by 2: **$x=4$**. Check: $2(4)+5=13$. ✓

> [!example]- Example B — variables both sides: $3x+2=x+10$
> Subtract $x$: $2x+2=10$. Subtract 2: $2x=8$. Divide by 2: **$x=4$**. Check: $14=14$. ✓

> [!example]- Example C — distribute: $2(x+4)=18$
> $2x+8=18$ → $2x=10$ → **$x=5$**. Check: $2(9)=18$. ✓

> [!example]- Example D — all five steps: $5(x-1)=2x+7$
> $5x-5=2x+7$ → $3x-5=7$ → $3x=12$ → **$x=4$**. Check: $15=15$. ✓

> [!warning] Common traps
> - Change **both** sides, never just one.
> - $-(-6)=+6$: rewrite "minus a negative" as "plus" first.
> - Distribute to every term: $2(x+4)=2x+8$.
> - Isolate the $x$-term before dividing.

## Practice
1. $5x-3=2x+9$
> [!question]- Solution
> $3x=12$ → **$x=4$**.

2. $4x+1=7x-8$
> [!question]- Solution
> $9=3x$ → **$x=3$**.

3. $2x-6=5x$
> [!question]- Solution
> $-6=3x$ → **$x=-2$**.

4. $3(x-2)+5=14$ *(stretch)*
> [!question]- Solution
> $3x-1=14$ → $3x=15$ → **$x=5$**.

5. $2(3-x)=x+9$ *(stretch)*
> [!question]- Solution
> $6-2x=x+9$ → $-3=3x$ → **$x=-1$**.
