# U1.3 Multiplying Polynomials

## Learning Objectives
- Perform math operations on polynomials.

## Core Concepts

Multiplying polynomials is very similar to multiplying multi-digit numbers. Each place value (or term) in one expression is multiplied by every other place value (or term) in the other expression, and the resulting products are added together.

To multiply a number or an expression by a polynomial, we use the **distributive property**. This property allows you to multiply each term of the first polynomial by each term of the second. After distributing, you add the resulting products and combine like terms to simplify the expression.

### The Laws of Exponents
When multiplying variable terms, recall the product rule for exponents. It states that when multiplying exponential expressions with the same base, you add the exponents:
$$x^m \cdot x^n = x^{m+n}$$

### Steps for Multiplying Polynomials
1. **Use the distributive property**: Multiply each term of the first polynomial by each term of the second.
2. **Combine like terms**: Group the terms that have the same variables and exponents.
3. **Simplify**: Add or subtract the coefficients of the like terms to find the final polynomial in standard form.

## Examples

### 1. Multiplying Single Terms (Monomials)
The easiest place to begin practice is multiplying single terms.

**Example**: Multiply $2x^2(3x^5)$.
- First, multiply the coefficients (the numerical part): $(2)(3) = 6$
- Next, multiply the variable part by adding the exponents: $(x^2)(x^5) = x^{2+5} = x^7$
- **Result**: $6x^7$

### 2. Multiplying a Monomial by a Binomial
**Example**: Multiply $3x(4x^2 + 5)$.
- Multiply each term of the binomial, $4x^2$ and $5$, by the monomial $3x$:
  $$3x \cdot 4x^2 + 3x \cdot 5$$
- Simplify the products:
  $$12x^3 + 15x$$
- **Result**: $12x^3 + 15x$

### 3. Multiplying a Monomial by a Trinomial
**Example**: Multiply $9x^2(-3x^3 - 4x + 2)$.
- Multiply each term of the trinomial by the monomial $9x^2$:
  $$9x^2 \cdot (-3x^3) + 9x^2 \cdot (-4x) + 9x^2 \cdot (2)$$
- Simplify by adding exponents:
  $$-27x^5 - 36x^3 + 18x^2$$
- **Result**: $-27x^5 - 36x^3 + 18x^2$

### 4. Multiplying Two Binomials
**Example**: Multiply $(x - 2)(3x + 1)$.
- Distribute the first term of the first binomial ($x$) to each term of the second binomial ($3x$ and $1$):
  $$x \cdot 3x = 3x^2$$
  $$x \cdot 1 = x$$
- Distribute the second term of the first binomial ($-2$) to each term of the second binomial ($3x$ and $1$):
  $$-2 \cdot 3x = -6x$$
  $$-2 \cdot 1 = -2$$
- The product of the two binomials is the sum of these products. Add the products and combine like terms to simplify:
  $$(x - 2)(3x + 1) = 3x^2 + x - 6x - 2$$
  $$= 3x^2 - 5x - 2$$

### 5. Multiplying a Binomial by a Trinomial
**Example**: Multiply $(x + 3)(x^2 - 2x + 4)$.
- You must distribute the binomial $x + 3$ over each term of the trinomial.
- Multiply each term of the binomial by each monomial in the trinomial:
  $$(x \cdot x^2) + (3 \cdot x^2) + (x \cdot -2x) + (3 \cdot -2x) + (x \cdot 4) + (3 \cdot 4)$$
- Simplify each term:
  $$x^3 + 3x^2 - 2x^2 - 6x + 4x + 12$$
- Combine like terms:
  $$x^3 + x^2 - 2x + 12$$

## Organizing Large Products: The Table Method

As the number of terms increases in polynomials that are being multiplied, it can be difficult to keep track of all the products without making an error. For example, a trinomial multiplied by a polynomial with four terms will have 12 products that need to be combined!

One way to organize the products is by creating a table (or grid) with rows and columns corresponding to each of the terms.

**Example**: Multiply $(x^2 - 9x + 6)(-2x^2 + 4x)$ using a table.
Since a 3-term polynomial (trinomial) is being multiplied by a 2-term polynomial (binomial), create a 3-by-2 table. Complete the table by finding the respective products of intersecting rows and columns.

| | $-2x^2$ | $4x$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **$x^2$** | $-2x^4$ | $4x^3$ |
| **$-9x$** | $18x^3$ | $-36x^2$ |
| **$6$** | $-12x^2$ | $24x$ |

- **Step 1**: Find the terms inside the table by multiplying row headers by column headers.
- **Step 2**: Add all the products together:
  $$-2x^4 + 4x^3 + 18x^3 - 36x^2 - 12x^2 + 24x$$
- **Step 3**: Combine like terms to simplify:
  $$-2x^4 + 22x^3 - 48x^2 + 24x$$

## Practice Problems

**1. What is the simplified product of $(x - 4)(x^3 - 2x^2 + 6)$?**
- Distribute $x$: $x^4 - 2x^3 + 6x$
- Distribute $-4$: $-4x^3 + 8x^2 - 24$
- Combine: $x^4 - 2x^3 - 4x^3 + 8x^2 + 6x - 24$
- **Answer**: $x^4 - 6x^3 + 8x^2 + 6x - 24$

**2. What is the simplified product of $(x^2 - 5)(x^2 - 5)$?**
- Distribute $x^2$: $x^4 - 5x^2$
- Distribute $-5$: $-5x^2 + 25$
- Combine: $x^4 - 5x^2 - 5x^2 + 25$
- **Answer**: $x^4 - 10x^2 + 25$
