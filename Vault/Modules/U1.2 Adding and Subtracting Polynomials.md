# U1.2 Adding and Subtracting Polynomials

## Learning Objectives
- **Perform math operations on polynomials:** Focus on combining like terms to add and subtract polynomial expressions correctly.

## Adding Polynomials

Now that we have defined polynomials and discussed how to perform math operations on terms with various exponents, we can begin adding polynomials together. 

The primary rule for adding polynomials is to **combine like terms**. 

### Definition: Like Terms
**Like terms** are terms that have the exact same variable (or variables) raised to the exact same power. 
If terms are like terms, you can add their coefficients together while keeping the variable part unchanged.

**Examples of Combining Like Terms:**
- $4x^2 + 5x^2 = 9x^2$ (Like terms, both have $x^2$)
- $2x - 6x = -4x$ (Like terms, both have $x^1$)

### Steps for Adding Polynomials
1. **Remove the parentheses:** Since you are adding, the signs of the terms inside the parentheses do not change.
2. **Reorder the terms:** Group the like terms together, usually ordering them from the highest power to the lowest power (standard form).
3. **Combine like terms:** Add the coefficients of the like terms.

### Example 1: Adding Polynomials
**Problem:** Add the polynomials $(4x^2 + 3x - 7)$ and $(-2x^2 + 7x + 11)$.

**Solution:**
1. **Remove parentheses and write as a single expression:**
   $$ (4x^2 + 3x - 7) + (-2x^2 + 7x + 11) $$
2. **Reorder the terms from highest to lowest power:**
   $$ 4x^2 - 2x^2 + 3x + 7x - 7 + 11 $$
3. **Combine like terms:**
   - For $x^2$: $4x^2 - 2x^2 = 2x^2$
   - For $x$: $3x + 7x = 10x$
   - For constants: $-7 + 11 = 4$
   
   **Final Answer:**
   $$ 2x^2 + 10x + 4 $$

### Example 2: Adding Polynomials with Missing Powers
**Problem:** Find the sum of $(-7x^3 + 3x^2 - 9x + 4) + (-2x^2 + 9x^3 + 5 - 8x)$.

**Solution:**
1. Remove parentheses:
   $$ -7x^3 + 3x^2 - 9x + 4 - 2x^2 + 9x^3 + 5 - 8x $$
2. Group like terms:
   $$ (-7x^3 + 9x^3) + (3x^2 - 2x^2) + (-9x - 8x) + (4 + 5) $$
3. Combine:
   $$ 2x^3 + x^2 - 17x + 9 $$

## Subtracting Polynomials

Subtracting polynomials is a very similar process to adding them. However, **special care must be taken with the polynomial that is being subtracted.** Every single term in the subtracted polynomial must be subtracted. This means the subtraction sign (or $-1$) must be distributed to every term inside the parentheses of the second polynomial.

### Steps for Subtracting Polynomials
1. **Rewrite the subtraction problem as an addition problem:** Change the subtraction sign between the polynomials to an addition sign.
2. **Distribute the subtraction sign:** Multiply every term of the polynomial being subtracted (the one after the minus sign) by $-1$. This flips the sign of every term inside the parentheses.
3. **Reorder the terms:** Group like terms together, ordering from highest power to lowest power.
4. **Combine like terms:** Add the coefficients of the like terms.

### Example 3: Subtracting Polynomials
**Problem:** Subtract the polynomials: $(4x^2 + 3x - 7) - (-2x^2 + 7x + 11)$.

**Solution:**
1. **Distribute the subtraction sign (multiply by -1):**
   $$ 4x^2 + 3x - 7 - (-2x^2) - (7x) - (11) $$
   $$ 4x^2 + 3x - 7 + 2x^2 - 7x - 11 $$
2. **Reorder the terms from highest to lowest power:**
   $$ 4x^2 + 2x^2 + 3x - 7x - 7 - 11 $$
3. **Combine like terms:**
   - For $x^2$: $4x^2 + 2x^2 = 6x^2$
   - For $x$: $3x - 7x = -4x$
   - For constants: $-7 - 11 = -18$
   
   **Final Answer:**
   $$ 6x^2 - 4x - 18 $$

### Example 4: Practice with Subtraction
**Problem:** Subtract the polynomials: $(-7x^3 + 3x^2 - 9x + 4) - (-2x^2 + 9x^3 + 5 - 8x)$.

**Solution:**
1. Distribute the negative sign to the second polynomial:
   $$ -7x^3 + 3x^2 - 9x + 4 + 2x^2 - 9x^3 - 5 + 8x $$
2. Group like terms:
   $$ (-7x^3 - 9x^3) + (3x^2 + 2x^2) + (-9x + 8x) + (4 - 5) $$
3. Combine:
   $$ -16x^3 + 5x^2 - x - 1 $$

### Example 5: Subtracting Polynomials of Different Degrees
**Problem:** Perform the indicated operation: $(-5x^2 - 10x + 3) - (3x^3 + 7x - 4)$.

**Solution:**
1. Distribute the negative sign:
   $$ -5x^2 - 10x + 3 - 3x^3 - 7x + 4 $$
2. Group and reorder terms (starting with highest degree $x^3$):
   $$ -3x^3 - 5x^2 + (-10x - 7x) + (3 + 4) $$
3. Combine:
   $$ -3x^3 - 5x^2 - 17x + 7 $$

## Application: Profit Function

Polynomials are often used to model real-world business applications like Revenue, Cost, and Profit. 

**Formula:**
$$ \text{Profit} = \text{Revenue} - \text{Cost} $$

### Example 6: Modeling Profit
**Problem:** If revenue is modeled by $R(x) = 250x - 2x^2$, and cost is modeled by $C(x) = 3000 + 150x + 3x^2$, what would the profit function be modeled by?

**Solution:**
1. Set up the subtraction:
   $$ \text{Profit} = (250x - 2x^2) - (3000 + 150x + 3x^2) $$
2. Distribute the negative sign to the cost polynomial:
   $$ 250x - 2x^2 - 3000 - 150x - 3x^2 $$
3. Group like terms:
   $$ (-2x^2 - 3x^2) + (250x - 150x) - 3000 $$
4. Combine:
   $$ -5x^2 + 100x - 3000 $$

**Final Profit Function:**
$$ P(x) = -5x^2 + 100x - 3000 $$
