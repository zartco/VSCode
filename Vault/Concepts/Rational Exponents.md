# Rational Exponents

Rational exponents are another way to represent roots. The general rule for converting any radical expression into an exponential expression (and vice-versa) is:
$\sqrt[r]{m^n} = m^{\frac{n}{r}}$
- The base of the radicand ($m$) remains the base of the rational exponent.
- The exponent of the radicand ($n$) becomes the numerator.
- The index of the radical ($r$) becomes the denominator.

> [!example] Quick example
> $\sqrt[3]{x^2}=x^{\frac{2}{3}}$.
