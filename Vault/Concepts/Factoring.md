# Factoring

Rewriting a polynomial as a product - the reverse of multiplying. Patterns: GCF, trinomials, difference of squares $a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b)$.

> [!example] Quick example
> $x^2+7x+12=(x+3)(x+4)$.
