# Like Terms

Particular terms in an algebraic expression are known as like terms if they contain exactly the same variables raised to exactly the same power. They can have different coefficients. Like terms can be combined by adding their coefficients. All constants are also like terms.

> [!example] Quick example
> $3x+5x=8x$ and $8xy + 4xy = 12xy$. However, $5xy$ and $5xy^2$ are not like terms.
