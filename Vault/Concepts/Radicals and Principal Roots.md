# Radicals and Principal Roots

All positive numbers have two square roots, a positive and a negative. The positive square root is referred to as the **principal square root**. The value inside of the square root symbol is called the **radicand**.

> [!example] Quick example
> $\sqrt{121}=11$ (11 is the principal square root, not -11).
