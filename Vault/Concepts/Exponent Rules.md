# Exponent Rules

Shortcuts for powers: product $a^m a^n=a^{m+n}$, quotient $a^m/a^n=a^{m-n}$, power $(a^m)^n=a^{mn}$, zero $a^0=1$, negative $a^{-n}=1/a^n$.

> [!example] Quick example
> $x^3\cdot x^5=x^{8}$.
