## Exponents — [[Exponent Rules]]
$a^m a^n=a^{m+n}$ · $\dfrac{a^m}{a^n}=a^{m-n}$ · $(a^m)^n=a^{mn}$ · $a^0=1$ · $a^{-n}=\dfrac1{a^n}$ · $a^{m/n}=\sqrt[n]{a^m}$

## Solving equations — [[Properties of Equality]]
Simplify each side → gather variables one side → numbers the other → divide to isolate → check.

## Factoring — [[Factoring]]
$a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b)$ · $a^2\pm2ab+b^2=(a\pm b)^2$ · trinomial: $x^2+(p+q)x+pq=(x+p)(x+q)$
