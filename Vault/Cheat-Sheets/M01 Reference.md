# M01 Reference — Algebraic Expressions

## Exponent rules
$a^m\cdot a^n=a^{m+n}$ · $\dfrac{a^m}{a^n}=a^{m-n}$ · $(a^m)^n=a^{mn}$ · $(ab)^n=a^n b^n$ · $a^0=1$ · $a^{-n}=\dfrac{1}{a^n}$

## Radicals
$\sqrt{a^2}=|a|$ · $\sqrt{ab}=\sqrt{a}\,\sqrt{b}$ · simplify by pulling perfect squares: $\sqrt{50}=5\sqrt{2}$ · principal root is the **non-negative** one.

## Rational exponents
$a^{m/n}=\sqrt[n]{a^m}=\left(\sqrt[n]{a}\right)^m$ — denominator is the root.

## Special products
$(a\pm b)^2=a^2\pm 2ab+b^2$ · $(a+b)(a-b)=a^2-b^2$

## Factoring patterns
GCF first · trinomial $x^2+(p+q)x+pq=(x+p)(x+q)$ · difference of squares $a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b)$
