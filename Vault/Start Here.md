Welcome! This vault is your editable study workspace. Two-minute orientation below.

## What's in here
- **Modules/** — one note per WGU module (M01–M14). M01 and M02 are full lessons; M03–M14 are outlines we flesh out as we reach them.
- **Concepts/** — small "index-card" notes for single ideas (e.g. [[Distributive Property]]), linked from the modules. Click one to see everywhere it's used.
- **Cheat-Sheets/** and [[00 Formula Cheat-Sheet]] — quick references for before tests.
- [[00 Dashboard]] — your home base · [[00 Study Plan]] — the 8-week schedule · [[Error Log]] — mistakes to review · [[Resources]] — links.
- **Exports/** — polished WGU-themed HTML/PDF I generate from any note for sharing or printing.

## First-time setup (once)
1. Install Obsidian (I can do this for you).
2. Open this folder as a vault: Obsidian → **Open folder as vault** → pick **WGU Precalculus Vault**.
3. Turn on a few community plugins (Settings → Community plugins → Browse):
   - **Spaced Repetition** — turns notes into flashcards.
   - **Dataview** — powers the Dashboard's "what to review" list.
   - **Tasks** — checkboxes with due dates for the Study Plan.
   - **Excalidraw** — hand-drawn diagrams (unit circle, etc.).
   - *(optional)* **Obsidian Git** — automatic backups.
4. The WGU theme is already on (a CSS snippet under `.obsidian/snippets`).

## How the notes work
- `> [!example]-` boxes are **collapsible** — click to reveal a worked solution.
- `[[Double-bracket links]]` jump between notes; the **graph view** (left toolbar) shows the whole web.
- `$x^2$` renders as math.
- Colored boxes are **callouts** (goal, example, warning, question).

## The two-document setup
- **This vault** = where you study and edit.
- **Main notebook** (`WGU_Precalculus_Notebook.html`) = the polished compilation; I update it on your direction with live course data.
- Need the pretty version to share or print? Ask me to **export** a module — you get the WGU-themed HTML/PDF in **Exports/**.
